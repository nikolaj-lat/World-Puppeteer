# WORLD-BIBLE.md — Hunter x Hunter Canon Map (per arc)

Source of truth: the manga, through the published Succession War chapters (~ch. 410). Anime (2011) used only for visual/tonal reference. Internal timeline anchored to **Year 0 = the 287th Hunter Exam** (Gon and Killua are 12). All ages/dates below are relative.

This document drives: world lore, NPC era-validity, Story Start state triggers (arc flags), spoiler scoping, and per-arc power baselines (NEN-DESIGN §3, §8).

---

## Global constants (true in every arc)

- **World**: six continents around the Lake Mobius interior is UNKNOWN to the public until the Election era; the "known world" is officially everything — in truth a small subcontinent inside Lake Mobius (Dark Continent lore: secret at V5 state level). Treat as deep lore, surfaced only in arcs 7-8.
- **Hunter Association**: ~600 licensed Hunters, license = near-total legal immunity + access; exam yearly with brutal attrition. Chairman: Isaac Netero (arcs 1-6).
- **Nen**: real, hidden from the general public. Taught under controlled conditions; "baptism" by aura-node opening. The six categories, Water Divination, vows/limitations per NEN-DESIGN.md.
- **Mafia community**: Ten Dons rule globally; families (Nostrade, etc.) beneath them.
- **Zoldyck family**: world-famous assassins of Kukuroo Mountain, Republic of Padokea. Public tourist attraction; nobody passes the Testing Gate.
- **Kurta massacre**: Year -5. Phantom Troupe slaughtered the Kurta clan for their Scarlet Eyes; the eyes circulate on the black market. Public fact: clan destroyed, eyes traded. NOT public: Troupe member identities.
- **Phantom Troupe (Gen'ei Ryodan)**: 13 members, spider tattoos with numbers, from Meteor City (a fact almost nobody knows). Meteor City: a scavenger city of the stateless; its existence is semi-known, its tie to the Troupe is not.

## Tone & content rules (all arcs)

Mature, matching source: graphic violence, torture, child endangerment treated seriously, moral ambiguity (Hunters are not heroes; the Association licenses monsters). Sexual content is part of the world's tone where it fits the fiction (Hisoka's predatory menace, Heavens Arena nightlife, mafia decadence, Kakin court intrigue); Voyage's platform filter is the sole guardrail for prohibited categories — narrator instructions never steer toward them (D-007).

## ARC 1 — Hunter Exam (Year 0)

**Start premise**: Aboard the ship/at Zaban City for the 287th Hunter Exam.
**State**: Netero chairman, alive, bored, strongest "living" Nen user of his era (public: strongest, period). Examiners: Satotz, Menchi, Buhara, Lippo. Examinees include Gon (#405), Kurapika (#404), Leorio (#403), Killua (#99), Hisoka (#44, returning psycho who mauled an examiner last year), Tonpa (#16, rookie crusher), Hanzo, Pokkle, Ponzu, Bodoro, and "Gittarackur" (Illumi in needle disguise).
**Canon beats**: Trick Tower, Zevil Island badge hunt, final tournament; Killua disqualified after killing Bodoro under Illumi's manipulation; Gon passes, confronts Illumi; Zoldyck rescue follows (Arc 2).
**Power baseline**: Nen-Unawakened (NEN-DESIGN tier 1). Hisoka/Illumi are walking spoilers of what awakened means.
**SPOILER WALL — narrator must not reveal**: Nen by name (only rumors of "trained auras"); Gittarackur = Illumi; Hisoka's category or Bungee Gum; Ging's identity/whereabouts/Zodiac seat; Troupe member identities; Netero's true power ceiling; Meteor City–Troupe link; Alluka's existence; anything Chimera Ant/Dark Continent.

## ARC 2 — Zoldyck Family (Year 0, weeks after exam)

**Start premise**: At the Testing Gate of Kukuroo Mountain, or inside as staff/family.
**State**: Killua home "grounded" after the exam; Gon/Kurapika/Leorio come to retrieve him. Mike (guard hound), gatekeeper Zebro, groundskeeper Seaquant; butlers: Gotoh, Canary, Amane, Tsubone. Family present: Silva, Zeno, Kikyo, Illumi (in/out), Milluki, Kalluto. **Alluka exists, sealed away — narrator never references her** (Election-arc reveal).
**Canon beats**: Gate weights (1st door 4 tons total), Canary's loyalty conflict, Killua released by Silva's bargain ("never betray your friends" / return when done).
**Power baseline**: Unawakened to Initiate (butlers are trained Nen users; family are masters).
**SPOILER WALL**: Alluka/Nanika; Illumi's needle in Killua's head; family Nen categories; Chrollo–Silva history surface-only ("father once fought a thief").

## ARC 3 — Heavens Arena (Year 0-1)

**Start premise**: Registering as a fighter at the 251-floor Heavens Arena.
**State**: Floor masters hold floors 230+; floor 200 is the Nen hazing line where "initiation" maims the ignorant. Wing (true teacher) and his student Zushi; Hisoka loiters as a floor-200 gatekeeper for amusement; lower-floor sharks Gido, Riehlvelt, Sadaso prey on newcomers.
**Canon beats**: Wing teaches Ten/Ren/Water Divination as the *honest* version of the "Hyakushiki-juu" lie; Kastro's doomed double; Hisoka vs Gon (floor 200); Hisoka reveals Bungee Gum's "qualities of both rubber and gum."
**Power baseline**: Initiate → Practitioner. This is the canonical Nen-learning arc — the Story Start where Water Divination plays on-screen.
**SPOILER WALL**: Troupe identities (Hisoka's spider tattoo is fake — deep secret); Kastro's exact mistake before the duel; Ging; everything later.

## ARC 4 — Yorknew City / Phantom Troupe (Year 1, September)

**Start premise**: Yorknew at auction season — as a hunter, mafia retainer, Troupe member, or chain-bearer.
**State**: Underground auction (Sept 1); Ten Dons alive (until they aren't); Nostrade family rising on Neon's "Lovely Ghostwriter" prophecies (her power is the family's secret engine); Kurapika newly chained (Holy Chain, Chain Jail, Judgment Chain; Emperor Time — Specialist via Scarlet Eyes), bodyguarding Neon under Light Nostrade; colleagues Melody, Basho, Squala (doomed), supervisor Dalzollene (doomed).
**Troupe roster (active)**: Chrollo (#0, Skill Hunter), Nobunaga (#1), Feitan (#2, Pain Packer/Rising Sun), Machi (#3), Hisoka (#4, fake member here for Chrollo), Phinks (#5), Shalnark (#6), Franklin (#7), Shizuku (#8), Pakunoda (#9), Bonolenov (#10), Uvogin (#11), Kortopi (#12). #4's original holder Omokage long gone; Kalluto joins as #4 *after* Hisoka quits at arc's end.
**Canon beats**: Uvogin vs Kurapika (Uvo dies); Troupe massacres the auction & fakes their deaths via Kortopi; Ten Dons hire assassins incl. Zoldycks, then rescind; Gon/Killua tail Pakunoda; Squala dies, Eliza grieves; hostage trade in Lingon Airport tunnel; Pakunoda spends her life on Memory Bomb; Chrollo's Nen sealed by Judgment Chain (cannot use Nen, cannot contact members); Hisoka leaves; Gon hears the Greed Island tape — first real Ging thread.
**Power baseline**: Practitioner. Troupe members are Adept-Master tier.
**SPOILER WALL**: Greed Island's nature (until tape); Kurapika's exact vow terms are HIS secret (narrator knows, NPCs don't); Chrollo's seal persists into later arcs; Meteor City origin revealed only at the Troupe's own scenes.

## ARC 5 — Greed Island (Year 1-2)

**Start premise**: Entering the game — bought in, smuggled in (Hisoka), or hired by Battera's clear-the-game contract (Tsezguerra's squad, Goreinu, Kazsule's mob).
**State**: Greed Island is a Nen-construct island in the real world (public believes it's a video game); created by Ging + 10 game masters (Razor, Eta, Elena, Dwun, List…). Cards, Book binder, spell economy; Bomber trio (Genthru, Sub, Bara) farming players for cards via Countdown.
**Canon beats**: "Bisky" reveals as Biscuit Krueger (57, double-star, true-form musclebound); brutal Ren/PoP training of Gon & Killua; dodgeball vs Razor (Hisoka allies, Bungee Gum visible); alliance politics; Gon vs Genthru (Rock through sacrifice play); "Accompany to Ging" → delivers them to **Kite** in the real world.
**Power baseline**: Practitioner → Adept (the training arc).
**SPOILER WALL**: Razor & masters' tie to Ging revealed in-game only; Kite connection only at the very end; Alluka still unknown; Ant foreshadowing zero.

## ARC 6 — Chimera Ant (Year 2-3)

**Start premise**: NGL border with Kite's expedition, or later: East Gorteau as Hunter taskforce / as an Ant.
**State & beats (compressed)**: Queen washes ashore in NGL (a Luddite narco-state run by Gyro); phagogenesis births humanoid soldier ants; squadron leaders (Colt/Kurt, Zazan, Rammot, Cheetu, Hagya/Leol, Meleoron…) defect as the Queen weakens; **Kite dies** vs Neferpitou; Gon/Killua's despair; Royal Guards born: Neferpitou (Terpsichora, Doctor Blythe), Shaiapouf (Beelzebub), Menthuthuyoupi (rage transformation). **Meruem** born premature, kills the Queen leaving; relocates to East Gorteau, Bizeff's puppet-dictatorship under Ming Jol-ik's corpse-regime; **Komugi** and Gungi reshape the King; Palace invasion (Netero, Zeno, Morel, Knov, Knuckle, Shoot, Ikalgo, Meleoron, Gon, Killua); Netero's Zero Hand fails → **Poor Man's Rose** suicide bomb; Meruem poisoned, dies blind with Komugi; **Gon's transformation** (sacrificial vow → adult Gon, one-shot Pitou) → comatose husk; Welfin/Hina/Bizeff aftermath; Kite reborn as Meruem's red-haired ant sibling.
**Power baseline**: Adept; Royal Guard/Meruem content is Master-tier+ (mythic NPC tiers).
**SPOILER WALL (within-arc, phase-gated)**: Meruem's birth before it happens; Rose's existence (state secret even after use); Gon's transformation until it happens; Kite's rebirth (very end); Pariston/Zodiacs not yet on-stage.
**Content note**: this arc is the violence ceiling — genocide, body horror, child soldiers (Ikalgo's charm against it). Keep it as dark as canon.

## ARC 7 — 13th Chairman Election (Year 3)

**Start premise**: Association HQ during the election circus / hospital vigil for Gon / Killua's race for Alluka.
**State**: Netero dead (will + election rules via Beans); **Zodiacs** (twelve): Pariston (Rat, vice-chair, magnificent bastard), Mizaistom (Ox), Kanzai (Tiger), Pyon (Rabbit), Botobai (Dragon), Gel (Snake), Saccho (Horse), Ginta (Sheep), Saiyu (Monkey), Cluck (Chicken), Cheadle (Dog), Ging (Boar — identity initially unrevealed). Kurapika (new Rat) and Leorio (new Boar) join **only at arc's end/DC prep** after Pariston and Ging vacate. Ging surfaces — revealed as Zodiac (Boar, quits), Gon's father, double-star Ruins Hunter.
**Canon beats**: Election rounds & Pariston's games; Leorio's punch (and vote surge); **Alluka/Nanika** revealed — wish-granting at escalating price; Illumi (with Hisoka) hunts Killua/Alluka; needle removed earlier in Ant arc empowers Killua (Godspeed already manifested); Nanika heals Gon; Ging vs Pariston endgame — Pariston wins, instantly resigns, Cheadle becomes 14th chairman; Gon meets Ging at the World Tree.
**Power baseline**: Adept-Master for the cast around the player; Gon himself is Nen-less post-healing (vow spent) — a poignant playable state.
**SPOILER WALL**: Dark Continent named only at the very end (Beyond's broadcast); Nanika's nature (Gas-Life form from the DC) never explained even in canon — keep mysterious; Saiyu is the Zodiac mole (post-arc secret).

## ARC 8 — Dark Continent Expedition / Succession War (Year 3-4) ⚠️ PARTLY EXTRAPOLATED

**Canon (published)**: Beyond Netero announces the DC expedition; V5 becomes V6 admitting **Kakin Empire** (Nasubi Hui Guo Rou's hereditary-dictatorship-as-young-democracy); the **Black Whale** (200k passengers) sails; **Succession War**: 14 princes, kill-or-be-killed by Nen beasts (parasitic, vow-born guardians), Kurapika bodyguards Woble (prince 14) for Queen Oito; Tserriednich (prince 4) — Scarlet Eye collector, serial murderer, terrifying Nen prodigy (Specialist, future-sight); Benjamin, Camilla, Zhang Lei, Halkenburg et al. scheme; Zodiacs aboard (Mizaistom guards Beyond; Saiyu the mole); **Troupe aboard** hunting Hisoka — who, after losing to Chrollo at Heavens Arena (post-Election, Nen restored via Abengane), died, self-revived with Bungee Gum heartbeat, and declared war: Kortopi and Shalnark already murdered. Five Calamities lore (Brion, Zobae, Hellbell, Ai, Pap); Five Threats; Gas-Life Ai ↔ Nanika.
**EXTRAPOLATED (flag in lore)**: anything aboard past ~ch. 410 and the continent landing itself — outcomes of the Succession War, calamity encounters, Beyond's true plan resolution. The Story Start's lore entry carries an explicit "speculative continuation — manga incomplete" marker (locked decision D-002/architecture).
**Power baseline**: Master; the deadliest sandbox — politics, sealed-ship paranoia, Nen beasts that ignore tier.
**SPOILER WALL (within-arc)**: prince Nen beasts' rules are discovered, not stated; Saiyu's treason; Hisoka's location aboard.
