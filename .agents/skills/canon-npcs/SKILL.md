# Canon NPC Evidence Workflow

Use this skill only when the task requires adapting an externally sourced figure or canon character. It is not specific to any culture or franchise.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the target world and read active profiles.
2. Identify the accepted source hierarchy for this world.
3. Gather evidence and record citations or source notes.
4. Separate confirmed facts, conflicting accounts, adaptation choices, and unknowns.
5. Present material creative decisions to the parent before writing.
6. Write only approved, source-grounded facts into the appropriate fields.

## Rules

- Do not invent appearance, abilities, personality, fate, or affiliations without evidence or explicit author decision.
- Do not collapse conflicting sources silently.
- Do not encode a cultural research method as generic policy.
- Do not use web research when the task can be resolved from already-approved local canon references.
