# World Director

Use this skill when the creator’s intent is still being discovered. Do not use it to reopen already approved creative decisions during execution.

## Modes

- **Discovery:** interview deeply, find hidden assumptions, surface contradictions, and turn discussion into a bounded creative brief.
- **Execution handoff:** preserve approved decisions and identify only material ambiguities that must return to the creator.
- **Review support:** identify gaps and contradictions without inventing replacement direction.

## Discovery Workflow

1. Resolve the target world with `.claude/scripts/resolve-world.cjs`.
2. Read the world marker, world override instructions, generic relevant skills, and active profiles.
3. Ask concrete questions about tone, sensory detail, contradictions, stakes, hidden history, and player experience.
4. Stop interviewing when the creator approves a brief or explicitly instructs execution.
5. Record the approved brief boundaries: intended outcome, affected domains, protected content, unresolved assumptions, and validation requirements.

## Interview Depth

Use these prompts during Discovery only. Do not apply them to bounded Execution or technical Review.

Start broad, then make the vision concrete:

- What tale are we spinning, and what genre does it complicate?
- What feeling should linger after play?
- What makes this world, character, place, or system unlike a generic version?

Probe emotional resonance:

- What emotion dominates this element?
- What should delight, unsettle, or surprise players?
- If this were music, color, texture, or taste, what would it be?

Seek productive contradictions:

- What seems one way but is secretly another?
- Where should expectations break?
- What is beautiful about the ugly parts, or unsettling about the beautiful parts?

Demand tangible detail:

- What does it smell, sound, or feel like?
- What posture, habit, tell, cost, scar, or local custom makes it specific?
- What does a local know that an outsider would miss?

Find hidden depth:

- What secret is withheld from casual observers?
- What history left a visible or social scar?
- Who benefits, who suffers, and what failure state is most interesting?

Ask uncomfortable questions when useful:

- What injustice is normalized?
- Where can good intentions cause harm?
- What moral ambiguity should remain unresolved?

Continue only until the brief is bounded enough for implementation. A creator may decide that enough detail has been provided; when they approve execution, preserve the decision instead of reopening it.

## Escalation

Return to the creator for ambiguity affecting lore, tone, identity, relationships, canon, continuity, protected content, timeline, or major player experience.

Minor mechanical ambiguity may be resolved from the approved brief and local rules.
